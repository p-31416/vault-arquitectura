The clay isthe base for the edit LoRA: keep it clean, monochrome, edge-legible, camera + footprint 1:1. The two rules that make or break the render GENERATIVE, not descriptive. Every move must make new structure appear. Never write"X becomes [what it already looks like]" — that recolors instead of transforming. Forceemergence: openings → tall parabolic voids; joints → material fans into ribbed vaultclusters; supports → branch like trees; flat soffits → chains of compression vaults. Thehighest-yield signal is material fanning/branching at the joints. REVEAL and POPULATE the interior. Remove glazing/void planes and open each bay into adeep programmed interior with people and program props at true ceiling scale, samenear-white clay. This is what makes the branching columns and vaults actually appear. Gaudí scale — morph-intensity dial (0–10, default 7) Sets how far surfaces and supports are re-sculpted. Fixed at every level: camera /perspective, footprint, floor count, program, bay rhythm. In this variant the scale is namedopenly in the emitted prompt. 0–2: supports rake, faces bow into shallow saddles, lintels arch slightly. 3–5: catenary arches, parabolic openings, undulating soffits, tapering supports. 6–8 (default 7): full catenary ribs; joints fan into vault clusters; tree-columns; openedvaulted interior; hyperboloid-throat openings. Buildable but unmistakably Gaudí. 9–10 — maximal: the mass dissolves into one continuous force organism at the intensity ofthe Sagrada Família nave or the Colònia Güell crypt — dendriform column-forests knitting intoa single fan/groin-vaulted canopy, every surface a doubly-curved ruled shell, oculi droppingzenithal light, bone-graded solidity. At 10 hold nothing back. Design DNA — Gaudí's constructive logic (this variant may say so) Anchor on how his buildings stand, and name the reference works when useful:inverted-catenary / funicular arches (Colònia Güell crypt, Casa Milà attic); ruled surfaces —hyperboloids, hyperbolic paraboloids, conoids, helicoids (Sagrada Família); leaning columns onthrust lines (Park Güell portico); tree/dendriform branching columns (Sagrada Família nave);equilibrated envelopes with no flying buttresses; thin Catalan tile compression shells;light/air carved into the fabric (hyperboloid window throats, hollow vault crowns, taperinglight-wells); bone-graded, load-following solidity. Still forbidden — the name must NOT drag in the decorative skin (this matters more here,since "Gaudí" strongly evokes it): mosaic / trencadís / broken tile; glazed or coloured ceramiccladding; polychrome finish; iron flourishes; figurative carving applied to the structure;dragon/creature imagery and creature-chimneys; stained glass.
