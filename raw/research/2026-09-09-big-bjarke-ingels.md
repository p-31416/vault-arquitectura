# BIG / Bjarke Ingels — legado y redes

## Resumen

Bjarke Bundgaard Ingels (Copenhague 1974) funda PLOT (2001) y BIG – Bjarke Ingels Group (2005, Copenhague/NY/Londres/Barcelona, 800+ personas). Legado: hedonistic sustainability / pragmatic utopianism, alquimia programática (Yes Is More 2009, Hot to Cold 2015, Formgiving 2020), obras-manifiesto CopenHill, 8 House, VIA 57 West, Google HQ, Dryline/BIG U. Narrador nativo (cómic, TED, Instagram) que convierte ficción en hechos construidos.

## Ejes con citas inline

### 1. Bio oficial y estructura estudio

- BIG People declara: "Bjarke Ingels is the founder of BIG. His work is driven by the belief that architecture can be a tool for turning fiction into fact - merging pragmatic solutions with utopian possibilities. Since founding BIG in 2005" [1].
- Misma ficha: "advocates for hedonistic sustainability, an approach where ecological responsibility and human enjoyment are not at odds, but mutually reinforcing. He has taught at Harvard, Yale, and Columbia, and is the author of Yes Is More" [1].
- Bio New European Bauhaus (PDF oficial UE): "practicing within Landscape, Engineering, Architecture and Product design" + "Over 2 decades [...] Hedonistic Sustainability – the idea that sustainable buildings [...] increase the quality of life" + "published three books: Yes is More, Hot to Cold and Formgiving" + "Danish knighthood Dannebrog and French Chevalier des Arts et des Lettres [...] Innovator of the Year 2011 WSJ [...] Times 100 2016" [2].
- Trayectoria: OMA Rotterdam → PLOT 2001 (con Julien De Smedt) → BIG 2005; 400 empleados 2016 → 550 (2020) → 800+ (2025) [3][4][1].
- BIG LEAP actual: "Bjarke Ingels Group of Landscape, Engineering, Architecture, Planning, and Products" [5].

### 2. Manifiestos y doctrina

- *Yes Is More: An Archicomic on Architectural Evolution* (2009, 30 proyectos, DAM Book Award 2010): "Architecture seems entrenched between two equally infertile fronts: either naively utopian or petrifyingly pragmatic" → tercer vía pragmatic utopian [6][3].
- BIG.dk proyecto Yes Is More: cita Ingels "I am a cartoonist at heart [...] A comic book is all about communicating action, movement, development, or even evolution" [7].
- *Hot to Cold: An Odyssey of Architectural Adaptation* (Taschen 2015, 711p, 60 casos): "presents 60 case studies in harsh climate conditions [...] from Arabian desert to Finnish tundra" [8]; expo National Building Museum Washington 2015 "takes visitors from hottest to coldest [...] 60 three-dimensional models suspended" [9][10].
- Cita Hot to Cold: "Architecture never happens in the clinical conditions of a lab. It is always responding to [...] context, culture, landscape, climate" — Ingels [10].
- TED Hedonistic Sustainability (TEDxEast may 2011, 715k plays): "architecture is luxurious, sustainable and community-driven [...] from factory chimney that blows smoke rings to ski slope atop waste plant" [11]; speaker page lista 4 TEDs (2009 warp-speed tales, 2011 hedonistic, 2019 floating cities/LEGO, 2019 Mars) [12].
- ArchDaily 2023-2026: "sustainability won't win if it isn't better designed and more enjoyable to live in" [13]; "Architecture as a Framework for the Life That We Want to Live" [13].

### 3. Obras-manifiesto verificadas oficial

- CopenHill / Amager Bakke (Copenhague 2019, 41.000 m2, Infrastructure): "new breed of waste-to-energy plant [...] economically, environmentally and socially profitable" + "roof [...] artificial year-round ski slope [...] 500m ski runs [...] hike trail and climbing walls" + fachada "stacked aluminum bricks [...] can function as planters" [14]; premios WAF Best Building 2021, ArchDaily Building of Year Industrial 2020 [14].
- 8 House (Copenhague 2010, 62.000 m2): "perimeter block that morphs into a knot [...] mixing retail, rowhouses, apartments [...] create added value" — Ingels [15]; AIA Honor Award 2012 "allows residents to bike [...] up to 10th level" [16]; Best Green Roof Scandinavia 1700 m2 [17].
- VIA 57 West / Courtscraper (NY 2016, 77.202 m2, Durst): "fusion of [...] courtyard and skyscraper [...] we don't have to limit choices [...] we get both" — Ingels [18]; ArchDaily Housing Building of Year 2017, AIA Housing Award [18].
- Otros citados oficial/prensa: VM Houses, Mountain Dwellings, Superkilen, Google Bay View/Silicon Valley HQ (con Heatherwick, "largest photovoltaic façade, geothermal, blackwater"), Dryline/BIG U (10-mile resilient waterfront), 2 WTC, LEGO House, Serpentine 2016, Oceanix floating city, Olympus Moon con NASA [2][4][19].
- Guardian 2020: "monuments for Instagram age – CopenHill [...] West 57th courtscraper [...] pragmatic utopianism and hedonistic sustainability" + crítica "lack of joy in cladding/panels transition screen→reality" (contrapeso) [4].
