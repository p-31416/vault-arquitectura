## Fuente 1 — Walter P Moore, «MARS Pavilion»
URL: https://www.walterpmoore.com/projects/mars-pavilion — snippet

- «The MARS Pavilion comprises 70 unique "wishbone" style components intricately bolted together… The ABB robots' precision and the fabric's freedom were coupled to achieve an organic geometry and adjustable concrete mold. Designed in collaboration with Form Found Design, the Pavilion is derived from a catenary structure using concrete in its most natural state: compression.»
- «the Pavilion awed onlookers in March 2017 at the Amazon MARS Conference (Machine learning, Automation, Robotics, Space exploration) in Palm Springs… The Pavilion and a video of the design process were on exhibit at the A+D Architecture and Design Museum in the Los Angeles Arts District until October 2017.»
- **Vínculo Gaudí explícito**: «the geometry is inverted so that the chain "hangs" upwards, harkening back to the world-renowned Spanish architect Antoni Gaudí's use of inverting parabolic, hyperbolic, and catenary masonry forms in many of his famous structures, notably the Sagrada Familia.»
- «The inversion also resulted in minimum tensile forces due to flexure. Helix Steel Micro Rebar… provided the necessary flexible strength… in conjunction with CTS Rapid Set Cement.»
- «The MARS Pavilion is the first robotically built concrete structure in southern California.»

## Fuente 2 — uau studio, «Shell in the Park» (Varna, Bulgaria)
URL: https://www.uau-studio.com/project/shell-in-the-park — snippet

- «The Seaside Garden in Varna connects the city and sea… uau studio envisions adding small structures throughout the park for vending, eating, and socializing, inspired by sea shells… These "shell" pavilions are made using sustainable concrete, forming thin, strong, self-supporting structures.»
- «Each Shell Panel is a 50 mm thick folded concrete element, combining structure and finish. Panels connect to form a simple kiosk, with rain naturally draining off. These modular pavilions can be expanded or customized, serving as temporary or permanent structures for various uses in the park.»
- «Three Shell panels with two connections forming an arch hence forming a simple 'kiosk' module. The slopped planes of the structure direct rainwater to fall naturally on each side so that there is no need to incorporate drainage strategy.»
- Material: Effix Design (hormigón de alta precisión en moldes de formas complejas).

## Fuente 4 — IAAC, «GAUDI'S CAT» (2024)
URL: https://blog.iaac.net/gaidis-ca/ — snippet

- «GAUDI'S CAT pavilion is inspired by the geometry of Antonio Gaudi – famous Catenary arches. The structure of the pavilion consists of a central space under the canopy and an open part above. Catenary arches are located in three parts of the pavilion, serving as entrances to it, and elements of Catenary arches are also used on the top of the pavilion to create a wave-like contrasting geometry effect.»
- «The main structural elements are panels made using 3D printing from cheapwood. The pavilion is covered with fabric and decorated with bright strips, reflecting the vibrancy and contrast of Barcelona.»
- Proyecto del Master in Advanced Architecture 01 2023-24 (curso Computational Design II con Rodrigo Aguirre y Akshay Madapura).

## Fuente 7 — Tensinet, «Tachkemoni canopies» (Amberes, 2001)
URL: https://tensinet.com/index.php/projects-database/projects/4754 — snippet

- «Laurent Ney designed two canopies in collaboration with architect Christiaan Poulissen: one small one for the kindergarten and one larger one for the primary school… to cover part of the playground.»
- «the most appropriate material to span and shelter 263m2 playground was a curved pre-tensioned polyvinyl chloride membrane. The membrane shape was found intuitively by using a hyperbolic paraboloid membrane tensioned against a looped tubular frame. In plan, the surface area of the small canopy is a circle of 12m diameter, the large one an ellipse of 16m by 21m.»
- Secuencia constructiva: «while tensioning the membrane against the frame, both the ring and the columns experienced significant displacement. The tops of the columns were displaced by 10cm. In practice this meant that they had to be initially fixed skew to the foundations. When the membrane had been completely tensioned, the columns, in their permanent state, turned out to be perfectly vertical.»
- «This holistic approach led to the simultaneous design of a shelter and a structural surface element.» Forma: anticlástica; permanente.

## Notas de síntesis escala urbana
- **Patrón 1 — membranas tensadas**: dominan sombreado de juegos, canopies escolares, pabellones temporales (MPavilion). Ventajas: luz, costo, velocidad, sin columna central. Clave técnica: pretensado + patterning + anclajes.
- **Patrón 2 — cáscaras ligeras modulares**: kioscos de hormigón plegado/impreso (Varna, Boves) y catenarias invertidas robóticas (MARS). Ventajas: permanencia, drenaje natural, huella mínima.
- **Patrón 3 — pabellones efímeros de catenaria**: Shellstar (stand de feria), GAUDI'S CAT, Catenary Symphonies, Ideas City — la catenaria como lenguaje de evento.
- **Gaudí urbano propio**: farolas Plaça Reial/Pla de Palau (1878-90) + banco Park Güell (Jujol) — el mobiliario urbano como primer encargo público del arquitecto.
