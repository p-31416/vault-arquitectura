## Resumen

Andrej Karpathy (Bratislava 1986, eslovaco-canadiense) es investigador y educador central del deep learning moderno: cofundador de OpenAI (2015-2017, 2023-2024), Director de AI/Tesla Autopilot Vision (2017-2022), creador de CS231n Stanford y de la serie Zero to Hero, fundador de Eureka Labs (2024, escuela AI-native) y desde 2026-05-19 miembro del equipo pretraining de Anthropic (Claude). Su legado combina papers visión+lenguaje (tesis 2016, DenseCap, Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments), conceptos Software 2.0 / vibe coding / Software 3.0, y pedagogía abierta masiva (YouTube, GitHub, blogs).

## Ejes con citas inline

### 1. Trayectoria oficial verificada

- Sitio personal `karpathy.ai` declara: "I like to train deep neural nets on large datasets" y resume "2023-2024 I came back to OpenAI where I built a new team working on midtraining and synthetic data generation. 2017-2022 I was the Director of AI at Tesla, where I led the computer vision team of Tesla Autopilot" [1].
- Mismo sitio detalla PhD 2011-2015 con Fei-Fei Li (Stanford Vision Lab), instructor principal del "first deep learning class Stanford - CS 231n", con crecimiento "150 enrolled in 2015 to 330 in 2016, and 750 in 2017" [1].
- Página Stanford `cs.stanford.edu/people/karpathy` confirma timeline Tesla/OpenAI/PhD y lista talks (ScaledML 2020, Autonomy Day 2019, Software 2.0 stack 2018) [2].
- Formación previa: BSc Toronto (cs+física, con Hinton), MSc UBC (con van de Panne), internships Google Brain 2011 / Google Research 2013 / DeepMind 2015 [1].
- Movimiento 2026 a Anthropic reportado por CNBC/Reuters: "will be part of the pretraining team" / "building a team focused on using Claude to accelerate pretraining research" [6][9]; El País ES lo resume para audiencia hispana [5].

### 2. Papers y aporte técnico

- Tesis PhD *Connecting Images and Natural Language* (2016) — paradigma end-to-end para image-sentence matching, captioning y region-annotation [1][2].
- *Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments for Generating Image Descriptions* CVPR 2015 Oral (Karpathy & Fei-Fei) — combinación CNN regiones + Bi-RNN oraciones + embedding multimodal [1][2].
- *DenseCap: Fully Convolutional Localization Networks for Dense Captioning* CVPR 2016 Oral (Johnson*, Karpathy*, Fei-Fei) [1].
- *Large-Scale Video Classification with CNNs* CVPR 2014 Oral — dataset Sports-1M (1.1M videos, 487 clases) [1][2].
- *PixelCNN++* ICLR 2017, *World of Bits* ICML 2017, *Visualizing and Understanding Recurrent Networks* ICLR 2016 Workshop [1].
- *ImageNet Large Scale Visual Recognition Challenge* IJCV 2015 (coautor, experimentos human accuracy) [1][2]; auto-definido "reference human for ImageNet" por competir contra ConvNet [1].
- Fundacionales previos: *Deep Fragment Embeddings* NIPS 2014, *Grounded Compositional Semantics* TACL 2013, *Emergence of Object-Selective Features* NIPS 2012 [1].

### 3. Docencia abierta y redes (legado pedagógico)

- CS231n: primera materia deep learning Stanford, notas/slides/syllabus abiertos + r/cs231n [1][2]; videos 2016 vistos >800k veces según TIME [7].
- *Neural Networks: Zero to Hero* — curso desde backprop (micrograd) hasta GPT desde cero en código; syllabus verificado: micrograd 2h25m, makemore, MLP, BatchNorm, Backprop Ninja, WaveNet, GPT, Tokenizer [3]; repo `karpathy/nn-zero-to-hero` MIT, 24k stars [11].
- GitHub `karpathy`: nanoGPT, nanochat, llm.c, llama2.c, micrograd, arxiv-sanity [12]; pet projects ConvNetJS, char-rnn, neuraltalk2, arxiv-sanity.com [1].
- Blogs: GitHub blog (más viejo), Medium (Software 2.0 nov 2017), Bear blog actual; posts clave *Unreasonable Effectiveness of RNNs* (2015), *A Recipe for Training Neural Networks* (2019), *Software 2.0* (2017) [1].
- YouTube `@AndrejKarpathy` doble track técnico/general + talks State of GPT Build 2023, Tesla AI Day 2021 [1].
- TIME100 AI 2024: "His biggest impact [...] may come not from his research but from his role as [...] foremost educators" [7].

### 4. Ideas-fuerza: Software 2.0 → Eureka → Software 3.0

- *Software 2.0* (2017): código escrito por optimización (pesos neuronales) vs Software 1.0 escrito por humanos [1][13].
- Eureka Labs (16-jul-2024): "AI-native school", simbiosis teacher+AI Teaching Assistant, primer producto LLM101n undergraduate [8][10]; Business Insider: personaliza educación vía AI assistant [14].
- 2025-2026: Software 3.0, context engineering, agentes — perfil bestblogs.dev sintetiza su pensamiento reciente (fuente secundaria, marcar no verificado fino) [15].
- Relevancia estudio: concepto `cerebro-digital-karpathy` + Software 2.0 como marco para ComfyUI/DirectML y vault-visual.
