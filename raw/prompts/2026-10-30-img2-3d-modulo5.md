> Tres prompts verbatim del módulo 5 (imagen → 3D), extraídos del export del tablero. Referencia para un futuro MVP de img2-3d. Los tres comparten el registro de la maquette: geometría pura, materiales desechados por el curso en favor de la forma.

## Extracción (verbatim del export)

### Prompt 1

Physical architectural scale model cast in raw concrete, displayed in professional studio environment. Focus on pure volumetric geometry and spatial relationships. Handmade brutalist maquette showing essential building form - no excessive detail, only fundamental massing and major openings. Rough concrete texture with visible formwork marks and slight surface imperfections. Neutral gray concrete color with subtle variations. Studio photography with soft diffuse lighting creating gentle shadows that emphasize volume. Minimalist presentation on white surface against neutral background. Architectural study model aesthetic - emphasizes three-dimensional form, proportions, and spatial composition. Tactile object quality with honest material expression. Museum exhibition photography style.

### Prompt 2

Convert the uploaded hyperrealistic image into a 3D Rhinoceros viewport screenshot. Abstract the volumes while maintaining the exact geometry and proportions of the original architecture. Exclude all super fine details, textures, and photorealistic materials. Render the building as a clean, digital 3D model in shaded or rendered view mode characteristic of Rhino software, with soft gray or white default materials and subtle ambient occlusion shadows. Display clear, clean edge lines (isocurves or structural edges) typical of a CAD viewport. Abstract all vegetation and entourage, general massing of trees and vegetation. The final image should look exactly like a work-in-progress digital 3D model viewport screenshot, focusing purely on volumetric composition and exact geometric form.

### Prompt 3

Transform this architectural image into a clean, untextured 3D model render of a standalone, holdable object — as if the subject were modeled in Rhino and exists as a discrete, physical maquette that could be picked up and rotated in your hand. Object definition (critical):  Treat the subject as a finite, closed 3D volume with clearly defined boundaries on ALL sides — top, bottom, left, right, front, AND back The object must have explicit thickness and depth — not a flat facade or relief, but a fully bounded mass with a clear back face Define sharp, terminating edges on all corners where the object ends — no surfaces that dissolve, fade, or extend infinitely If the source is a facade or partial view, intelligently close it off into a complete, self-contained block/mass with logical depth (e.g., a facade becomes a slab-like volume with thickness; a building becomes a sealed massing study) Think: architectural maquette, Rhino "Cap" command applied, watertight mesh, 3D print–ready solid  Geometry:  Crisp, hard edges at every corner and surface intersection Clean planar surfaces with visible extrusion on all detail elements (window reveals, balconies, ornamentation, openings) Preserve original proportions and architectural features faithfully All silhouettes must be unambiguous and sharply defined against the background  Framing & isolation (critical):  The object must be fully contained within the image with generous empty margin on all sides — approximately 15–20% padding between the object's outermost edges and the image border The object must NOT touch, bleed into, or be cropped by any image edge Center the object in the composition Render from a three-quarter perspective view (rotated ~20–30° off-axis horizontally and tilted ~10–15° downward) so depth, side faces, and top are all clearly visible — this reveals the object's three-dimensionality and aids image-to-3D reconstruction  Material & surface:  Single uniform matte clay material across the entire object — light neutral gray (#C8C8C8 to #D5D5D5) No textures, no patterns, no color variation, no material differentiation Smooth, diffuse, slightly chalky surface — classic Rhino/Blender clay render aesthetic  Lighting:  Soft, even studio lighting with subtle key light from upper-front-left Gentle ambient occlusion in corners and recesses to enhance depth and edge readability Soft, contained cast shadow directly beneath the object (small, tight — reinforces that it's a discrete object sitting in space) No harsh contrast, no specular highlights, no reflections  Background:  Pure flat white background (#FFFFFF) Fully isolated subject, no ground plane texture, no context, no surroundings, no people, no vegetation, no atmosphere  Critical constraints:  Output must read unambiguously as a single, complete, holdable 3D object floating in white space — like a product photo of a clay maquette in a lightbox Every edge of the object must be visible and terminate cleanly within the frame No artistic interpretation, no stylization, no environmental effects
